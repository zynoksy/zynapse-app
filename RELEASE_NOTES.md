# Development status

This repository contains product information and support. App distribution is managed separately from GitHub.

## Android preview

The current preview includes keyboard-aware setup, the first-time app guide, the warm paper/clay interface, habit tracking, coping tools, journaling, progress, companion interactions and notification/progress fixes.

Internal emulator checks cover setup, saved data after restart, the app guide, companion interactions, and notification handling after progress resets and deletion. These checks do not establish broad device support or a stable public launch.

## Current limits

Physical-device testing, broader locale and cosmetic combinations, production account sync, purchase/advertising setup, and complete backup/restore remain unverified or incomplete. Some craving counters differ across screens. No iOS or desktop release is verified.

See [data guidance](guides/BACKUP_AND_RESTORE.md) and [rights](RIGHTS.md).
