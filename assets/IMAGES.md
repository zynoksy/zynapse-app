# Product images

## Cover and social preview

- `images/zynapse-cover.webp` is the 1024 × 500 illustrated cover, generated with ImageGen using the earlier Zynapse cover as a reference. The scene shows the orange fox on a winding autumn path toward a torii gate.
- `images/zynapse-social-preview.png` is a 1280 × 720 export of the earlier cover. Its original generation prompt is in [COVER_PROMPT.txt](COVER_PROMPT.txt).
- Artwork provenance and package notices are described in [RIGHTS.md](../RIGHTS.md).

## Android screenshots

The five WebP files in `screenshots/` show Home, Progress, Craving Support, Journal, and the fox companion. They were captured on an isolated Android emulator using Zynapse 1.0.0 closed-test build 17. The profile, streak, and journal entries use demo data. The full-screen captures were resized to 720 × 1280 and compressed for the README.
